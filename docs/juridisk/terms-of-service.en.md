## 1. Who we are

TIKIT is a ticket marketplace operated by **Din Russetid AS** (org. no. 936 491 243), [address], [support e-mail].

The organizer of each event is the **seller** of the tickets and responsible for the event itself. TIKIT arranges the sale, delivers the tickets and collects payment on the organizer's behalf. The organizer is named on the event page and on your receipt.

## 2. Account

You sign in with Vipps, Apple or Google and are responsible for activity on your account. If you are under 18, you must pay with money you are free to spend yourself, such as your own earnings or pocket money.

## 3. Purchase and price

- The price shown before you pay is the total price, including VAT where applicable.
- It consists of the ticket price and a TIKIT service fee of NOK 5 + 3.5 % of the ticket price, max NOK 49 per ticket. Free tickets have no fee.
- Tickets are held for you for 10 minutes, and for up to 30 minutes from the order while you pay. If payment isn't completed in time, the tickets are released to others.
- The organizer may set a maximum number of tickets per person for an event. The limit covers all your purchases together.
- Popular ticket drops may use a queue. Everyone who joins before the sale opens gets a place by lottery; later arrivals join at the back.
- The purchase is binding once payment is approved. You get a receipt by e-mail and the tickets in the app.

## 4. Payment

You pay with Vipps or card. Card payments are handled by Stripe, and TIKIT never sees card numbers. The amount is reserved and captured when the tickets are issued. If tickets sell out before your payment completes, you are not charged, and any reserved amount is released or refunded.

## 5. Right of withdrawal, cancellations and refunds

- **No right of withdrawal.** Tickets for events on a specific date are exempt from the right of withdrawal under the Norwegian Cancellation Act (angrerettloven) § 22 (m).
- **The organizer's refund rule.** The organizer decides whether tickets can be refunded, and until when: never, 7 days before, 48 hours before, or until the start. The rule is shown on the event page before you buy. When you refund yourself, you get the ticket price back, but not the service fee.
- **Cancellation.** If the event is cancelled, you automatically get the full amount back, including the service fee.
- **Postponement or significant change.** If the event is moved or significantly changed and you don't want to keep your ticket, you are entitled to a refund. Contact the organizer or TIKIT.
- **Payout.** Refunds go back to the payment method you used. It may take 3–5 business days before the amount shows.

## 6. The ticket

- Your ticket is a live QR code in the app that refreshes regularly. Screenshots and copies are not valid, and each ticket can be used once.
- If you show the ticket number instead of the QR code, you must show ID.
- The organizer may ask for ID and refuse entry for breaches of age limits or house rules.
- Personal tickets are valid only for the person named on them.

## 7. Transfers and resale

- You can transfer tickets in the app when the organizer allows it. The ticket then gets a new code and a new number, and the old ones stop working.
- Resale happens only through TIKIT and never above what you paid, in line with the Norwegian act prohibiting mark-ups on resale of tickets for cultural and sports events.
- If your ticket is sold, you get the sale price back to the payment method you used, never more than you paid.
- Tickets sold or transferred outside TIKIT may be blocked.

## 8. Misuse

We may block accounts and cancel purchases resulting from fraud, automated buying, buying for resale at a profit, or other misuse. The payment for a cancelled purchase is then refunded.

**Content and reporting.** Only organizers approved by TIKIT can publish events. Content that is offensive, hateful, illegal, misleading or encourages harmful use of alcohol or drugs is not allowed. Anyone can report an event or an organizer in the app. We handle reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these terms and close organizers who do so repeatedly. You can also hide an organizer so its events are no longer shown to you.

## 9. Liability

- The organizer is responsible for the event: its content, delivery, safety and entry.
- TIKIT is responsible for delivering tickets correctly and for handling payments and refunds as described here.
- We are not liable for indirect losses such as travel or accommodation unless caused by our gross negligence.
- Nothing in these terms limits your rights under mandatory consumer law.

How we process personal data is described in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.en.md).

## 11. Changes to these terms

A purchase is governed by the terms in force when you bought. Changes apply only to purchases made after they are published.
